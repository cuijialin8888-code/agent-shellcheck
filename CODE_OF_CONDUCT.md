# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a focused, welcoming place to improve the quality of
agent instructions. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss technical ideas
with evidence, and make room for people with different platforms and levels of
experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or any
conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is
representing the project. Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's
private vulnerability/reporting channel when available. Maintainers may edit,
hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions will aim to be proportionate, documented, and respectful
of reporter privacy.
